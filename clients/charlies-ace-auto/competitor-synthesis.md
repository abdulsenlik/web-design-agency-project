# competitor synthesis

charlie's ace auto & body service, concept "the art of getting you back"
source: competitor-matrix.md only (7 sites, studied 2026-10-23, read-only, no screenshots, so visual scores are medium to low confidence).
"our site" lines come from the design brief. items marked (planned) are not checked against the live build.

the field averages 6.0 out of 10 and scores lowest on interaction (4.9). electrified garage (7.9) is the only real design benchmark. that gap is the lane for the scroll film.

## best ideas borrowed

- **electrified garage: say what you will not do.** it states its limits plainly (when the manufacturer is the better route, that diagnostic time is billed). our site: process claims are written in plain words ("we explain what we found before work starts", "nothing gets fixed until you say go"), and they stay off until charlie confirms them. no superlatives.
- **mike's auto body north: specific craft proof beats adjectives.** its best copy names equipment and who signs off the paint. our site: act two of the film (inspection) makes the "explain before work" step the proof. we name no equipment, sign-off or certification until charlie confirms it.
- **protek bathgate: explain "your right to choose your repairer".** it teaches the visitor that they, not the insurer, pick the shop. our site: the faq answers choosing your own body shop in illinois, in calm general terms, pending charlie's ok.
- **gmw perth: objection-led faq and plain, repeated service labels.** it answers "does servicing outside the dealer void the warranty" and uses the same five plain labels everywhere. our site: the faq covers magnuson-moss warranty, and the service list uses plain names (collision repair, dent repair, brakes, diagnostics and warning lights) with the editorial voice saved for headlines.
- **alpha collision: recognise the accident moment.** its "i was in a car accident" choice speaks to someone in a bad week before they know the service name. our site: the film opens on the damaged sedan (act one, the problem) and the body-collision page speaks to that moment in calm copy, not alarm (planned).
- **xtreme auto collision and protek: a low-friction estimate with a visible path.** xtreme's photo estimate avoids typing on a phone, protek prints a three step path next to the form (send a photo, we reply, you decide). our site: one estimate form saved to the site's own database and read on a password-protected /inbox page, photos optional and few required fields (planned), with signal red kept for that one action.

## mistakes deliberately avoided

- **mike's auto body north: a headline on the same idea as ours.** its hero sells repair as an art driven by passion, right beside "the art of getting you back". our site: leads with the return, not the word art. graphite and ivory editorial look plus the 4 act film, not dark luxury video.
- **alpha collision: unfinished and unsourced content shipped live.** lorem ipsum, a "coming soon" gallery, a stats row with no source, "24/7" next to weekday hours. our site: there is no /our-work page until real project photos exist, no ratings or stats shown, and no hours shown until verified ("call ahead for today's hours").
- **protek bathgate: before and after pairs that are not the same car.** its labels are mismatched and its before/after gallery has no pairs, which destroys the proof. our site: no before and after claims at all. the film is captioned "Illustrative film. Not a customer vehicle." and real pairs come only as same car, same angle, with the owner's permission.
- **xtreme auto collision: four equal-weight buttons in the hero.** estimate, authorization, appointment and call all compete. our site: one primary action, and signal red #C62F2F is reserved for it.
- **gmw perth: empty trust headings, no stated reply time, registration required up front.** it has a "certified excellence" heading with nothing under it, says only "promptly", and asks for the registration number. our site: no certification or badge headings (none are confirmed), the plate is not required (planned), and the reply time is shown only once charlie picks one.
- **electrified garage: no on-page form and no "what happens next".** its requests go to a third-party quote form, and the tone is purely technical. our site: the estimate form lives on the site itself, the next step is shown after sending (planned), and the copy keeps the precision but adds warmth for neighbors in a bad week.
